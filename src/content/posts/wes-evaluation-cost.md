The **WES evaluation cost** for US admissions is **$239** for a Course-by-Course evaluation with WES ICAP, about **NPR 36,200**. With standard delivery it comes to **$253 (about NPR 38,300)**. A Document-by-Document ICAP evaluation costs **$171**. For Canadian immigration, the WES **ECA** costs **C$264 plus 13% HST**, about **C$298 (NPR 32,400)**, before delivery. These are the fees on [wes.org](https://www.wes.org/evaluations-and-fees/education/graduate-admissions/), checked on **6 November 2026**. Rupee amounts use Nepal Rastra Bank's selling rate that day: **NPR 151.47 per US dollar** and **NPR 108.54 per Canadian dollar** ([NRB](https://www.nrb.org.np/forex/)).

Ready to apply? Follow the [WES application guide](/wes-application-from-nepal/).

## WES evaluation fees for the USA

| Evaluation | WES ICAP | WES Basic |
|---|---|---|
| **Course-by-Course** | **$239** (about NPR 36,200) | $186 (about NPR 28,200) |
| **Document-by-Document** | **$171** (about NPR 25,900) | $118 (about NPR 17,900) |

The prices don't include delivery or other fees ([WES fees](https://www.wes.org/evaluations-and-fees/education/graduate-admissions/)).

## Extra WES fees: delivery, copies and changes

| Extra | Fee |
|---|---|
| Standard delivery (no tracking) | $14 (about NPR 2,100) |
| International courier, per address | $86 (about NPR 13,000) |
| US courier, per address (1-day / 2-day / 3–5 business days) | $42 / $28 / $19 |
| Sealed envelope, each | $9 |
| Extra copy of the report, ordered with your application | $58 |
| Report ordered after the evaluation is complete | First report $58 with Basic, $35 with ICAP; each additional $35 |
| Upgrade from Basic to ICAP while the evaluation is in progress | $53 |
| Update a completed evaluation (add a degree or course, Document-by-Document to Course-by-Course, or upgrade to ICAP) | $92 |
| Cancel a file or application | $56 processing fee |

The cancellation fee is from [WES Help](https://us-help.wes.org/support/solutions/articles/14000126395-is-there-a-cancellation-fee-); a completed evaluation can't be cancelled or refunded. The rest are from the WES fee page.

## WES ECA cost for Canada (Express Entry)

For Express Entry and other Canadian immigration programmes you need WES's **Educational Credential Assessment (ECA)** ([WES: ECA](https://www.wes.org/eca-2/)):

| | Fee |
|---|---|
| ECA | **C$264** (about NPR 28,700) |
| ECA + 13% HST | **C$298.32** (about NPR 32,400) |
| Standard delivery | C$14 |
| Courier to the US or abroad, per address | C$97 |
| Next-day courier, Canada only | C$29 |

WES says the ECA price "does not include delivery fees, additional fees, or a 13% Harmonized Sales Tax (HST)". The ECA includes an electronic report for you, access to the report for IRCC, an official paper report (delivery fees apply) and storage of your report and verified transcripts. For **admission to a Canadian university** (not immigration), check what your university asks for and WES Canada's own price list before you pay.

## What a WES evaluation costs in total from Nepal

| You are | You pay WES | In rupees |
|---|---|---|
| Applying for a master's in the USA (Course-by-Course ICAP + standard delivery) | $253 | about NPR 38,300 |
| Applying for Express Entry in Canada (ECA + HST) | C$298.32 + delivery | about NPR 32,400 + delivery |
| Applying to a US college that accepts Document-by-Document ICAP (+ standard delivery) | $185 | about NPR 28,000 |

On top of the WES fee, budget for:

- **Your university's fee** for transcripts or for sending records to WES. Kathmandu University, for example, sends electronic copies directly to WES after an online request, for a fee ([KU](https://exam.ku.edu.np/?p=4811)).
- **A courier from Nepal**, if your university gives you a sealed envelope to send.
- **Your card's foreign-currency charges.** WES is paid online in dollars, so you need a card that works for international payments.

## WES ICAP vs Basic: is ICAP worth $53 more?

| | WES ICAP | WES Basic |
|---|---|---|
| Evaluation report | Yes | Yes |
| Your verified transcripts sent with the report | **Yes** | No |
| Transcripts stored for later reports | Yes | No |
| Course-by-Course price | $239 | $186 |
| Document-by-Document price | $171 | $118 |
| Report ordered later | $35 | $58 |

**ICAP** (International Credential Advantage Package) sends copies of your verified documents to your recipients along with the report, and stores your transcripts and report after the evaluation, so later reports are easier and cheaper ([WES: benefits of ICAP](https://www.wes.org/wes-international-credential-advantage-package/)). WES marks it "Recommended" on its fee page, and many universities require it. **Basic** is the evaluation report only. For most Nepali students ICAP is worth it: it costs $53 more, and if you apply to more universities later each new report is cheaper, so you don't have to order transcripts from Nepal again. If you chose Basic, you can upgrade to ICAP for $53 while the evaluation is still in progress.

## Course-by-Course vs Document-by-Document

| | Document-by-Document | Course-by-Course |
|---|---|---|
| What it shows | Each qualification and its US equivalent | Every course, with credits, grade and level |
| GPA | No | Yes, on the US 4.0 scale |
| Usually asked for | Some undergraduate admissions, some employers | Graduate admissions, licensing boards |
| ICAP price | $171 | $239 |

If you are applying for a master's or PhD, or the university mentions a GPA, you most likely need Course-by-Course. Your university's instructions always win.

## How long does a WES evaluation take?

WES only starts once **all** your documents have arrived ([WES processing times](https://www.wes.org/processing-times/), checked 6 November 2026):

| Evaluation | Document verification | Evaluation |
|---|---|---|
| ECA for Canadian immigration | Typically 2 weeks | Typically 1 week (up to 2 if complex) |
| Document-by-Document | Typically 2 weeks | Typically 1 week (up to 2 if complex) |
| Course-by-Course | Typically 2 weeks | Typically 2 weeks (up to 4 if complex) |

Add the time your university needs to send transcripts and any courier time from Nepal. Paying for a faster courier for **your report** doesn't speed up the evaluation itself.

## Estimate your WES GPA for free

A Course-by-Course report converts your Nepali grades into a GPA on the US 4.0 scale, using WES's own rules for each country and institution. A simple "percentage divided by 25" often gives a different answer. Before you pay, try WES's free [iGPA calculator](https://apps.wes.org/igpa-calculator/); the GPA in your final report is what counts.

## How long is a WES evaluation valid?

For **Canadian immigration**, your ECA must be **less than 5 years old** when you create your Express Entry profile and when you apply ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/education-assessed/how.html)). For **university admission** there is no single rule: each university decides how old a report it accepts.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a WES evaluation cost?** For US admissions, Course-by-Course costs $239 with WES ICAP or $186 with Basic, and Document-by-Document costs $171 with ICAP or $118 with Basic. Delivery is extra, from $14 for standard post. These are WES's fees as of 6 November 2026.

**How much is WES evaluation in Nepali rupees?** A Course-by-Course ICAP evaluation ($239) is about NPR 36,200, or about NPR 38,300 with standard delivery, at Nepal Rastra Bank's selling rate of NPR 151.47 per dollar on 6 November 2026. Your bank's card rate may differ slightly.

**How much does the WES ECA cost for Canada?** C$264, plus 13% HST, which makes C$298.32, about NPR 32,400 at NRB's 6 November 2026 rate. Delivery is extra: C$14 standard or C$97 by courier outside Canada.

**What is the difference between WES ICAP and Basic?** ICAP sends your verified transcripts with the evaluation report and stores them, so later reports cost $35. Basic is the report only, and later reports cost $58. ICAP costs $53 more, and many universities require it.

**Is the WES fee the same for every university?** The evaluation fee is the same, but each extra recipient, copy and courier delivery adds to it. Choose the evaluation type your university asks for, because changing it later costs $92.

**How long does a WES evaluation take?** Typically 2 weeks to verify your documents after they all arrive, then 1 week for an ECA or Document-by-Document evaluation, or 2 weeks for Course-by-Course, up to 4 if complex. Transcripts from Nepal add time on top.

**Can I get a WES evaluation for free?** No. Every WES evaluation has a fee. Some universities accept other evaluators or assess transcripts themselves, so check your university's requirements before you pay.

**Can I get a refund from WES?** You can cancel a file or application for a $56 processing fee, but a completed evaluation can't be cancelled and its fees aren't refunded. Confirm which evaluation you need before you pay.

**How do I pay WES from Nepal?** Online, in dollars (or Canadian dollars for an ECA), so you need a card that works for international online payments. Keep the receipt and your WES reference number.

**Is a WES evaluation valid for 5 years?** The 5-year limit is for Canadian immigration: your ECA must be under 5 years old when you create your Express Entry profile and when you apply. Universities set their own rules for how old a report can be.
