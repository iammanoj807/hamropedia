For US admissions, the **WES evaluation cost** is **$239** for a Course-by-Course evaluation with WES ICAP, roughly **NPR 36,200**, or **$253 (about NPR 38,300)** once you add standard delivery. A Document-by-Document ICAP evaluation is **$171**. For Canadian immigration, the WES **ECA** costs **C$264 plus 13% HST**, about **C$298 (NPR 32,400)**, before delivery. All fees come from [wes.org](https://www.wes.org/evaluations-and-fees/education/graduate-admissions/), checked on **6 November 2026**. To show the cost of a WES evaluation in Nepal, we converted them at Nepal Rastra Bank's selling rate for that day: **NPR 151.47 per US dollar** and **NPR 108.54 per Canadian dollar** ([NRB](https://www.nrb.org.np/forex/)).

When you're ready to apply, follow the [WES application guide](/wes-application-nepal/).

## WES evaluation fees for the USA

| Evaluation | WES ICAP | WES Basic |
|---|---|---|
| **Course-by-Course** | **$239** (about NPR 36,200) | $186 (about NPR 28,200) |
| **Document-by-Document** | **$171** (about NPR 25,900) | $118 (about NPR 17,900) |

Delivery and other fees come on top ([WES fees](https://www.wes.org/evaluations-and-fees/education/graduate-admissions/)).

The cancellation fee comes from [WES Help](https://us-help.wes.org/support/solutions/articles/14000126395-is-there-a-cancellation-fee-), and a completed evaluation can't be cancelled or refunded. Every other fee here is from the WES fee page.

## WES ECA cost for Canada (Express Entry)

Express Entry and other Canadian immigration programmes need WES's **Educational Credential Assessment (ECA)** ([WES: ECA](https://www.wes.org/eca-2/)):

| | Fee |
|---|---|
| ECA | **C$264** (about NPR 28,700) |
| ECA + 13% HST | **C$298.32** (about NPR 32,400) |
| Standard delivery | C$14 |
| Courier to the US or abroad, per address | C$97 |
| Next-day courier, Canada only | C$29 |

As WES puts it, the ECA price "does not include delivery fees, additional fees, or a 13% Harmonized Sales Tax (HST)". You get an electronic report, access to the report for IRCC, an official paper report (delivery fees apply) and storage of your report and verified transcripts. If you want **admission to a Canadian university** rather than immigration, check what the university asks for and WES Canada's own price list before you pay.

## WES evaluation cost in Nepal: what you pay in total

| You are | You pay WES | In rupees |
|---|---|---|
| Applying for a master's in the USA (Course-by-Course ICAP + standard delivery) | $253 | about NPR 38,300 |

| Applying to a US college that accepts Document-by-Document ICAP (+ standard delivery) | $185 | about NPR 28,000 |

Beyond the WES fee, set aside money for:

- **Your university's fee** for transcripts or for sending records to WES. Kathmandu University, for instance, sends electronic copies straight to WES after an online request, for a fee ([KU](https://exam.ku.edu.np/?p=4811)).
- **A courier from Nepal**, if your university hands you a sealed envelope to send.

## WES ICAP vs Basic: is ICAP worth $53 more?

| | WES ICAP | WES Basic |
|---|---|---|
| Evaluation report | Yes | Yes |
| Your verified transcripts sent with the report | **Yes** | No |
| Transcripts stored for later reports | Yes | No |
| Course-by-Course price | $239 | $186 |
| Document-by-Document price | $171 | $118 |
| Report ordered later | $35 | $58 |

**ICAP** (International Credential Advantage Package) sends copies of your verified documents to your recipients with the report, and keeps your transcripts and report on file afterwards, so later reports are simpler and cheaper ([WES: benefits of ICAP](https://www.wes.org/wes-international-credential-advantage-package/)). WES labels it "Recommended" on its fee page, and many universities insist on it. **Basic** gives you the report and nothing else. For most Nepali students ICAP is worth the extra $53: if you apply to more universities later, each new report costs less, and you won't have to order transcripts from Nepal all over again. Already chose Basic? You can upgrade to ICAP for $53 while the evaluation is still in progress.

## Course-by-Course vs Document-by-Document

| | Document-by-Document | Course-by-Course |
|---|---|---|
| What it shows | Each qualification and its US equivalent | Every course, with credits, grade and level |
| GPA | No | Yes, on the US 4.0 scale |
| Usually asked for | Some undergraduate admissions, some employers | Graduate admissions, licensing boards |
| ICAP price | $171 | $239 |

Applying for a master's or PhD, or does the university mention a GPA? Then you almost certainly need Course-by-Course. Your university's instructions always come first.

## WES GPA calculator: estimate your GPA for free

A Course-by-Course report turns your Nepali grades into a GPA on the US 4.0 scale, following WES's own rules for each country and institution, so the quick "percentage divided by 25" trick often gives a different number. Try WES's free [iGPA calculator](https://apps.wes.org/igpa-calculator/) before you pay; only the GPA in your final report counts.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a WES evaluation cost?** For US admissions, Course-by-Course costs $239 with WES ICAP or $186 with Basic, and Document-by-Document costs $171 with ICAP or $118 with Basic. Delivery is extra, starting at $14 for standard post. These are WES's fees as of 6 November 2026.

**How much is WES evaluation in Nepali rupees?** A Course-by-Course ICAP evaluation ($239) comes to about NPR 36,200, or about NPR 38,300 with standard delivery, at Nepal Rastra Bank's selling rate of NPR 151.47 per dollar on 6 November 2026. Your bank's card rate may differ a little.

**How much does the WES ECA cost for Canada?** C$264, plus 13% HST, which makes C$298.32, about NPR 32,400 at NRB's 6 November 2026 rate. Delivery costs extra: C$14 standard, or C$97 by courier outside Canada.

**What is the difference between WES ICAP and Basic?** ICAP sends your verified transcripts with the evaluation report and stores them, so later reports cost $35. Basic is just the report, and later reports cost $58. ICAP costs $53 more, and many universities require it.

**Is the WES fee the same for every university?** The evaluation fee itself doesn't change, but every extra recipient, copy and courier delivery adds to it. Choose the evaluation type your university asks for from the start, because changing it later costs $92.

**How long does a WES evaluation take?** Typically 2 weeks to verify your documents once they've all arrived, then 1 week for an ECA or Document-by-Document evaluation, or 2 weeks for Course-by-Course, up to 4 if complex ([WES processing times](https://www.wes.org/processing-times/), checked 6 November 2026). Transcripts from Nepal add time, and paying for a faster courier for your report doesn't speed up the evaluation itself.

**Can I get a WES evaluation for free?** No. Every WES evaluation has a fee. Some universities accept other evaluators or assess transcripts themselves, so check your university's requirements before you pay.

**Can I get a refund from WES?** You can cancel a file or application for a $56 processing fee, but a completed evaluation can't be cancelled and its fees aren't refunded. Make sure you know which evaluation you need before you pay.

**How do I pay WES from Nepal?** Online, in dollars (or Canadian dollars for an ECA), so you need a card that works for international online payments; your card may add its own foreign-currency charges. Keep the receipt and your WES reference number.

**Is a WES evaluation valid for 5 years?** The 5-year limit applies to Canadian immigration: your ECA must be under 5 years old when you create your Express Entry profile and when you apply ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/education-assessed/how.html)). Universities set their own rules on how old a report can be.
