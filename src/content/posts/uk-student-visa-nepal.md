The rules for a **UK student visa from Nepal** have shifted a lot since the government's May 2025 immigration white paper, and two more changes land within the next two months. This guide sets out what actually applies in 2026: the requirements, the **UK student visa fee**, the money you must show in your account, and the cost in Nepali rupees. It keeps those rules apart from changes that were only proposed, and every number links to its official source.

> **Update (5 November 2026):** for applications made **on or after 30 November 2026**, the money you must show rises to **£1,203 a month** outside London and **£1,570 a month** in London ([UKCISA](https://www.ukcisa.org.uk/news/student-update-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-september-2026/)). From **1 January 2027**, the Graduate visa also falls to **18 months** for most graduates ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa)).

## UK student visa rules 2026: the short version

| Rule | What applies | Since |
|---|---|---|
| Student visa application fee | **£558** | 8 April 2026 |
| Immigration Health Surcharge (students) | **£776 per year** | 6 February 2024 |
| Money to show, outside London | **£1,203 per month**, up to 9 months (£1,171 before 30 Nov 2026) | 30 November 2026 |
| Money to show, London | **£1,570 per month**, up to 9 months (£1,529 before 30 Nov 2026) | 30 November 2026 |
| Graduate visa length (bachelor's, master's) | **2 years** if you apply on or before 31 Dec 2026; **18 months** from 1 Jan 2027 | Confirmed Oct 2025 |
| Graduate visa, "high-compliance" universities | **2 years** even after January 2027 (Budget policy; university list not yet published) | Announced 28 Oct 2026 |
| Graduate visa (PhD) | 3 years (unchanged) | — |
| Bringing dependants | Only for postgraduate research courses (or some government-funded scholarships) | Courses starting from January 2024 |
| Working during term | Usually up to **20 hours a week** on a degree-level course | — |

## 1. How much money you need to show (bank balance)

You have to prove you can cover your course fees and your living costs. How much you need for living costs depends on **the date you apply**:

| Where you study | Apply before 30 Nov 2026 | Apply on or after 30 Nov 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Outside London | £1,171 a month | **£1,203 a month** |
| London | £1,529 a month | **£1,570 a month** |

You show this for up to **9 months**, plus any first-year tuition you haven't paid yet ([UKCISA, September 2026](https://www.ukcisa.org.uk/news/student-update-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-september-2026/); [gov.uk: Student visa, money](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/money)).

**The 28-day rule:** the money has to sit in the account for **at least 28 days in a row**, and that 28-day period must end **within 31 days of the date you apply** ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/money)). If the balance drops below the amount on even one day, the 28 days start over.

**Example, applying from Nepal on or after 30 November 2026:** for a course outside London you need 9 × £1,203 = **£10,827** for living costs. At Nepal Rastra Bank's selling rate on 5 November 2026 (NPR 204.71 to £1), that's about **NPR 22.2 lakh**, plus any unpaid tuition. In London it's 9 × £1,570 = **£14,130**, about **NPR 28.9 lakh**. The rate moves every day, so check the [NRB exchange rate](https://www.nrb.org.np/forex/) when you prepare your bank statement.

## 2. UK student visa fee and health surcharge

- **Student visa fee:** **£558**, whether you apply from outside the UK or extend or switch inside it ([gov.uk: Student visa](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa)). Before 8 April 2026 it was £524.
- **Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS):** **£776 per year** for students and their dependants, paid up front for the whole visa. A part year of 6 months or less costs half, £388 ([gov.uk: healthcare surcharge](https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application/how-much-pay)).

Because your visa usually runs a few months past the end of your course, a one-year master's often means paying IHS for more than a year: 1 year and 4 months, for instance, is £776 + £388 = **£1,164**. The official [IHS calculator](https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application/how-much-pay) gives your exact figure.

## 3. Total cost of a UK student visa from Nepal

For a typical one-year master's outside London, applying on or after 30 November 2026 (rupee figures at NPR 204.71 to £1, NRB's selling rate on 5 November 2026):

| Item | GBP | About NPR |
|---|---|---|
| Visa application fee | £558 | 1.14 lakh |
| Health surcharge (1 year 4 months) | £1,164 | 2.38 lakh |
| **Visa + surcharge** | **£1,722** | **3.53 lakh** |
| Living costs to show in your account | £10,827 | 22.2 lakh |
| Unpaid first-year tuition | your CAS amount | — |

Add the TB test, translations and courier costs on top. And to send tuition or living costs out of Nepal, you'll also need a [No Objection Certificate (NOC)](/noc-nepal-apply-online/) from the education ministry.

## 4. UK student visa requirements for Nepalese students

- **A CAS** (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) from a licensed university or college.
- **The money** in your account for 28 days, as explained above.
- **English at CEFR level B2** for a degree-level course (B1 below degree level), shown through an approved test or your university's own assessment, depending on your sponsor ([gov.uk: knowledge of English](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/knowledge-of-english)).
- **A TB test certificate.** Nepal is on the list of countries whose nationals need one for visas over 6 months ([gov.uk: TB test](https://www.gov.uk/tb-test-visa/countries-where-you-need-a-tb-test-to-enter-the-uk)). It's valid for **6 months** from your chest X-ray and must come from an approved clinic in Nepal.
- **Your academic documents**, as listed on your CAS.
- **Preparation for a [credibility interview](/top-ukvi-interview-question-examples-and-answers/)**: universities and UKVI often interview Nepali applicants about their course, money and plans.

**When to apply:** from outside the UK, up to **6 months** before your course starts. A decision usually takes about **3 weeks** ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa)).

## 5. The Graduate visa (PSW) is getting shorter

The **Graduate visa**, which many Nepali students call **PSW** (post-study work), lets you stay and work in the UK after a UK degree without needing a job offer. It lasts **2 years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026** and **18 months if you apply on or after 1 January 2027**; PhD graduates still get 3 years. You apply from inside the UK, and it costs **£937** plus the health surcharge ([gov.uk: Graduate visa](https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa)).

The October 2026 Budget says graduates of **"high-compliance" universities** will keep two years, but no list has appeared yet. All the details: [UK Graduate visa: 18 months or 2 years?](/uk-graduate-visa-two-years-high-compliance-universities/)

## 6. Working while you study

On a degree-level course you can usually work up to **20 hours a week in term time** and full time during official vacations, unless your CAS or visa says otherwise ([gov.uk: Student visa](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa)).

## Other changes from the September 2026 rules

From **8 October 2026**, the Statement of Changes of 3 September 2026 also tightened the deadlines for giving your **biometrics** and changed the rules on **fee waiver** dates (UKCISA). The UK is also rejoining **Erasmus+** from 2027.

## What was proposed but is not (yet) a student rule

The [May 2025 immigration white paper](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/restoring-control-over-the-immigration-system-white-paper) floated several other changes, including:

- **tougher compliance thresholds** for universities that sponsor international students;
- a **levy on universities' income from international students**;
- making **10 years**, not 5, the normal qualifying period for settlement (permanent residence). **Update (2 November 2026):** the government has since confirmed this, starting in December 2026 and running through 2027, and time on Student and Graduate visas won't count towards settlement. See [UK ILR changes: 5-year vs 10-year routes](/ilr-changes-uk-5-year-vs-10-year-route-earned-settlement/).

Some of these still need legislation or consultation, and the details keep moving. **Treat them as proposals until they appear in the official Immigration Rules or on gov.uk.**

## Frequently asked questions

**What are the new UK student visa rules for 2026?** The main changes are higher money requirements from 30 November 2026 (£1,203 a month outside London, £1,570 in London), the £558 visa fee since April 2026, and the Graduate visa dropping to 18 months for applications from 1 January 2027.

**How much bank balance is required for a UK student visa from Nepal?** Your unpaid first-year tuition plus 9 months of living costs. If you apply on or after 30 November 2026, that's £10,827 outside London (about NPR 22.2 lakh) or £14,130 in London (about NPR 28.9 lakh), held for 28 days in a row.

**How much does a UK student visa cost from Nepal?** The visa fee is £558 (about NPR 1.14 lakh). With the health surcharge, a one-year master's usually comes to about £1,722 (about NPR 3.5 lakh), before living costs and tuition.

**Is IELTS compulsory for a UK student visa?** No single test is compulsory. A degree course needs English at B2 level; many universities accept IELTS or another approved test, and some assess you themselves. Check what your university's CAS says.

**How much GPA is required to study in the UK from Nepal?** The visa itself has no GPA rule. Each university sets its own entry grades, so check the page for your course.

**Can I bring my husband or wife on a UK student visa?** For courses starting from January 2024, only if you're on a postgraduate research course such as a PhD, or on some government-funded scholarships ([gov.uk: Student visa, family members](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/family-members)).

**What about the Graduate visa after my course?** See [UK Graduate visa: 18 months or 2 years?](/uk-graduate-visa-two-years-high-compliance-universities/) for the dates, the fee and the exceptions.
