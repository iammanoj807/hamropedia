Applying for a **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** is one of the first official steps for any Nepali student going abroad. The good news: the whole application is online. This guide walks through every screen, with screenshots, and starts with what has changed in 2026.

> **Update:** In April 2026, Nepal's new government published reform plans that include overhauling the NOC process. One proposal would approve foreign exchange only for courses in "priority" sectors such as STEM, health and IT. **These are proposals, not rules in force.** As of this update, the NOC is still applied for online as described below ([The PIE News, 7 April 2026](https://thepienews.com/nepal-proposes-scrapping-current-noc-process-in-sweeping-reform-push/)). 2026 reports also say the service is now fully "faceless", with no visit to the ministry needed ([Techpana](https://techpana.com/2026/156253/nepal-digitizes-noc-service-ends-in-person-requirements-for-students)). Always check the official portal, [noc.moest.gov.np](https://noc.moest.gov.np/), before you apply.

## What a NOC is for

The NOC is issued by Nepal's education ministry. It is mainly needed to **send money out of Nepal for your education**:

- **Bank payments:** banks ask for the NOC before transferring tuition fees abroad.
- **Currency exchange:** you need it to convert Nepali rupees into foreign currency for your studies.
- **One institution per NOC:** money can only be sent to the institution named on your NOC. Changing institution or country means applying for a new NOC.
- **Official record:** it shows the government has no objection to you studying abroad at that institution.

## Documents you need

Have clear scans ready before you start. Images must be under **5 MB** each.

- **Offer or acceptance letter**: the form accepts an offer letter, acceptance letter, I-20 (USA), COE or COA.
- **Academic documents**: transcripts and certificates (the form asks for notarised copies).
- **Citizenship certificate**: front and back combined into a single image.
- **Passport** (if you have one).
- **A recent passport-size photo**.
- **University or college invoice** (if you don't have one, the form lets you upload the offer letter instead).
- **Language test result** (IELTS, PTE, TOEFL and so on), if you have one.

## Step-by-step: the online NOC application

The screenshots below are from a real application. Personal details are blurred. The portal's look may change slightly over time, but the steps stay the same.

### 1. Enter and verify your mobile number

Go to [noc.moest.gov.np](https://noc.moest.gov.np/), choose **Apply for NOC**, enter your mobile number and click **Proceed**. A one-time token arrives by SMS; enter it to verify your number.

![NOC Online home page with the mobile number field](/images/posts/noc/step-1-1.webp "Step 1: enter your mobile number")

![Entering the OTP token sent by SMS](/images/posts/noc/step-2-1.webp "Enter the token from the SMS")

### 2. Create your password

As a new user you are asked to set a password. Type it twice and click **Submit**.

![Creating a password on the NOC portal](/images/posts/noc/step-3-1.webp "Step 2: set a strong password")

### 3. Verify your email

Enter your personal email address and click **Request Token**. Check your inbox (and spam folder), paste the token, and click **Verify Email**.

![Email verification on the NOC portal](/images/posts/noc/step-4-1.webp "Step 3: request an email token")

![Entering the email verification token](/images/posts/noc/step-5-1.webp "Paste the token and verify")

### 4. Citizenship setup

Enter your citizenship number, date of birth (BS), issuing district and issue date exactly as on your certificate, then click **Find**. Fill in your name, gender, parents' names and permanent address, and upload your citizenship scan (front and back in one image) and photo.

> **Tip:** Your date of birth here is in Bikram Sambat. If you need to check a BS ↔ AD date, use the [Nepali date converter](/tools/nepali-date-converter/).

![Citizenship setup form](/images/posts/noc/step-6-1.webp "Step 4: citizenship details")

![Address and document upload fields](/images/posts/noc/step-6-2.webp "Permanent address and scans")

### 5. Passport setup

Enter your passport number and date of birth (AD), and upload a scan. If you don't have a passport yet, tick **I do not have passport yet**. Click **Confirm**.

![Passport setup form](/images/posts/noc/step-7-1.webp "Step 5: passport details")

### 6. Academic background and study plan

Choose the country and level of your previous study, then your **destination country**, **institution**, **level** and **subject**. If your institution is missing, use **Request New**; the NOC team verifies and adds it, which can take a few days.

![Previous academic details](/images/posts/noc/step-8-1.webp "Step 6: previous study")

![Choosing destination institution, level, stream and subject](/images/posts/noc/step-8-2.webp "Pick your institution, level, faculty and subject")

Then upload your documents: offer letter (or I-20/COE), notarised academic transcript and certificate, university invoice, and any other supporting documents. Choose **Self** or **Studying through Consultancy**, tick the declaration, and click **Confirm**.

![Uploading NOC documents](/images/posts/noc/step-8-3.webp "Upload documents and confirm")

### 7. Review and submit

Check everything carefully. **You cannot edit the application after submitting.** Click **Proceed**, then download the form for your records.

![Review screen before submitting](/images/posts/noc/step-9-1.webp "Step 7: review, then proceed")

![Application submitted successfully](/images/posts/noc/step-10-1.webp "Download a copy of your form")

## After you apply

### Track your status

Open **My NOC** from the menu to see your application. The status starts as **Not Seen / Waiting Verification**.

![The My NOC menu](/images/posts/noc/step-11.webp "Find your application under My NOC")

![NOC application status waiting for verification](/images/posts/noc/step-12-1.webp "Status: waiting for verification")

### Pay the fee once verified

When the status changes to **Application Verified / Waiting Payment**, open the application. You will be asked to pay the **NRs 2,000** revenue fee using the voucher number shown, through the online payment option or at a bank with the printed voucher. After payment, your NOC is issued and can be downloaded from the portal.

![Application verified and waiting for payment](/images/posts/noc/step-13-1.webp "Status: verified, waiting payment")

![Payment instruction for the NRs 2,000 fee](/images/posts/noc/step-14-1.webp "Pay the NRs 2,000 fee using your voucher number")

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Name mismatch.** Use your name exactly as on your academic certificates and citizenship. Small spelling differences cause rejections.
- **Wrong date format.** Citizenship dates are BS; passport dates are AD. Don't mix them up.
- **Blurry or oversized scans.** Keep each file under 5 MB and make sure text is readable.
- **Wrong institution.** Money can only go to the institution on your NOC, so select the exact campus that issued your offer.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long does a NOC take?** It varies. It is often quoted as a few working days, but it can take longer around intake peaks or if your institution has to be added first. Apply as soon as you have your offer letter.

**Do I need a new NOC to change university?** Yes. The NOC is tied to one institution.

**Can a consultancy apply for me?** Yes, the form has a "Studying through Consultancy" option, but with this guide you can do it yourself.
