The **IRD PAN search** is the Inland Revenue Department's free page for checking a PAN number in Nepal: [ird.gov.np/pan-search](https://ird.gov.np/pan-search/). Type the PAN number, tick the captcha and click search. It needs no login and works for a **PAN no search** of your own number, a **PAN ID search** or a quick **PAN card check** of a business. This guide shows how to do a **PAN number search** on the website and in the IRD Nepal app. It also explains why you can't do a **PAN search by name**, and what to do if you have forgotten your own PAN.

## Quick answer: PAN search in Nepal

| You want to | Where | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| Check a PAN number | [ird.gov.np/pan-search](https://ird.gov.np/pan-search/) | The PAN number, then the captcha |
| Check a PAN on your phone | The **IRD Nepal** app ([Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.prixa.irdnepal), [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ird-nepal/id1475260354)) | The PAN number |
| Check a tax clearance | [ird.gov.np/tax-clearance](https://ird.gov.np/tax-clearance/) | PAN, fiscal year and tax clearance number |
| Find a PAN you forgot | The office that holds your PAN | Your citizenship certificate |
| Get a new PAN | [PAN card registration](/pan-card-registration-nepal/) | Your NID and NID mobile number |

## How to do an IRD PAN search on ird.gov.np

1. Open **[ird.gov.np/pan-search](https://ird.gov.np/pan-search/)**. From the IRD homepage, click **<span lang="ne">PAN नम्बर खोजी</span>** in the menu or **<span lang="ne">स्था.ले.नं खोजी</span>** in the row of quick links.
2. Type the PAN number in the **<span lang="ne">स्थाई लेखा नम्बर</span>** box (1). It takes digits only.
3. Tick the captcha ("I'm not a robot").
4. Click **<span lang="ne">खोज्नुहोस्</span>** (Search) (2). To clear the box and start again, click **<span lang="ne">रिसेट गर्नुहोस्</span>** (Reset).

![IRD PAN search page with the PAN number box and the search button marked](/images/posts/pan/pan-3-pan-search.webp "IRD PAN search: type the PAN number, tick the captcha, then search")

That is the whole **www ird gov np pan search**: there is one box, for the PAN number, and no login. The search is free. People also call it a **PAN card search** or **PAN ID search**, because the PAN number is your tax ID, but it is the same page.

## PAN search on your phone: the IRD Nepal app

IRD has an official app called **IRD Nepal**, for [Android (Google Play)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.prixa.irdnepal) and [iPhone (App Store)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ird-nepal/id1475260354). Both are linked from the bottom of ird.gov.np. IRD's banner for the app lists what you can do in it, including:

- **PAN Search** (<span lang="ne">खोजी</span>)
- tax clearance check (<span lang="ne">करचुक्ता खोजी</span>)
- personal PAN (PPAN) registration
- filing the D-01 income return and zero-transaction VAT returns
- IRD notices and tax information

Download it only from Google Play or the App Store through the links on ird.gov.np, so you get IRD's own app.

## Can you do a PAN search by name?

No. IRD's official PAN search has a single box, and it only accepts the **PAN number**. There is no field for a name, a citizenship number or a phone number, so an **IRD PAN search by name** isn't possible on IRD's page.

Be careful with websites or Facebook pages that offer to find someone's PAN by name. They are not IRD. To find your own forgotten PAN, use the official routes in the next section.

## Forgot your own PAN number? How to find it

- **Check what you already have:** your PAN card, and the SMS and email IRD sent when you registered online.
- **Print it again:** IRD's FAQ says a lost personal PAN card can be printed again from your **taxpayer login** ([IRD FAQ](https://ird.gov.np/faq/)).
- **Ask the office:** if you don't remember the number, the FAQ says to take your **citizenship certificate** to the office that holds your PAN and ask them to search for it. Once they find it, you apply there to get your PAN again.

The [office list](https://ird.gov.np/contact-info/) is on ird.gov.np. If you registered online with your NID, your PAN is held at the Inland Revenue Office you chose during registration.

## Why do a PAN check?

- **After you register:** check that your new PAN number is in IRD's records before you give it to an employer or a bank.
- **Before you deal with a business:** if a shop, contractor or supplier gives you a PAN number, check it on IRD's page first.
- **For your own records:** keep the PAN number with your documents. Anyone who earns income such as a salary, wages or house rent must have a personal PAN, under IRD's **"PAN for all"** policy ([IRD FAQ](https://ird.gov.np/faq/)).

## IRD tax clearance search

IRD's site also has a **tax clearance search** (<span lang="ne">कर चुक्ता खोजी</span>) at [ird.gov.np/tax-clearance](https://ird.gov.np/tax-clearance/), linked from the IRD homepage. It asks for three things: the **PAN** (<span lang="ne">स्थाई लेखा नम्बर</span>), the **fiscal year** (<span lang="ne">आर्थिक वर्ष</span>) and the **tax clearance number** (<span lang="ne">कर चुक्ता न.</span>). Use it to check a tax clearance certificate that someone shows you.

## Don't have a PAN yet?

If you have an NID, you can get a personal PAN online on IRD's new taxpayer portal. The [PAN card registration guide](/pan-card-registration-nepal/) shows every step with screenshots, including how to print your PAN card.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I do an IRD PAN search?** Go to ird.gov.np/pan-search, type the PAN number in the स्थाई लेखा नम्बर box, tick the captcha and click खोज्नुहोस्. It is free and needs no login. You can also search a PAN in IRD's official IRD Nepal app on Android or iPhone.

**Is a PAN ID search the same as a PAN search?** Yes. Your PAN is your tax ID number, so a PAN ID search, a PAN card search and a PAN number search all mean checking the number on ird.gov.np/pan-search. Type the number, tick the captcha and click खोज्नुहोस्.

**Can I search PAN by name in Nepal?** No. IRD's official PAN search only accepts the PAN number, with no field for a name or citizenship number. If you forgot your own PAN, check your PAN card or IRD's SMS and email, or ask the office that holds your PAN.

**How do I find my PAN number if I forgot it?** Look at your PAN card or the SMS and email from IRD. IRD's FAQ says you can print a lost personal PAN card from your taxpayer login, or take your citizenship certificate to the office that holds your PAN and ask them to search for it.

**Is the IRD PAN search free?** Yes. The page at ird.gov.np/pan-search has no fee and no login. You only type the PAN number and tick the captcha. Be wary of other sites that charge money or ask for your details to "check" a PAN.

**Can I check a PAN number on my phone?** Yes. Open ird.gov.np/pan-search in your phone's browser, or install IRD's official IRD Nepal app from Google Play or the App Store. IRD's banner for the app lists PAN Search among its services.

**What is the difference between a PAN search and a tax clearance search?** A PAN search checks a PAN number. A tax clearance search, at ird.gov.np/tax-clearance, checks a tax clearance certificate. It needs the PAN, the fiscal year and the tax clearance number printed on the certificate.
