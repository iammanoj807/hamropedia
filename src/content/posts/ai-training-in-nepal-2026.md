From high-school students in Dhangadhi to anyone with an internet connection, AI training is reaching more people in Nepal. Here are the programmes in the news, and how to make the most of them.

## Free online course: "Effective Use of AI and Prompt Writing"

The **National Vocational Training Establishment** is running a **free online training** on the effective use of AI and prompt writing on **4–5 November 2026**, with **2,000 seats** in total ([Nepal News, 1 November 2026](https://english.nepalnews.com/s/main-left/nepal-news-evening-briefing-sunday-november-01-2026/)). It is a good first step if you have never used AI tools for study or work.

## "AI for Nepal 2026" starts in Dhangadhi

The national **"AI for Nepal 2026"** campaign was launched in **Dhangadhi, Sudurpashchim Province**, where **more than 400 Grade 11 and 12 students** received hands-on training in AI tools, data analytics basics, spotting AI-enabled fraud and preventing cybercrime. It is organised by the **Computer Association Nepal (CAN) Federation, Sudurpashchim**, with Chief Minister **Khagraj Bhatta** as chief guest ([ICT Frame, 1 October 2026](https://ictframe.com/ai-for-nepal-kicks-off-in-dhangadhi/)).

"Our youth must not remain mere passive consumers of global AI products," said Chiranjibi Adhikari, acting central president of CAN Federation, describing the campaign as a nationwide movement for technological self-reliance.

## AI literacy classes for citizens

In **Lalitpur**, a three-day pilot **AI literacy** programme in June, run with Lalitpur Metropolitan City and **Open Knowledge Nepal**, taught residents to use AI for tasks such as translation, writing and accessing government services ([The Himalayan Times](https://thehimalayantimes.com/kathmandu/ai-literacy-pilot-training-begins-in-lalitpur)). A Kathmandu Post feature on how Nepalis are using AI in daily life, from cooking to bank records, covered the closing of a Lalitpur AI literacy programme where **UN Volunteers** provided training and mentoring; one participant found it too basic ([The Kathmandu Post, 25 October 2026](https://kathmandupost.com/science-technology/2026/10/25/from-induction-cookers-to-bank-records-nepalis-find-ways-to-use-ai)).

## 7th Annual Nepal AI School (28 Dec 2026 – 7 Jan 2027)

For students who want to go deeper, the **NepAl Applied Mathematics and Informatics Institute for research (NAAMII)** runs the **7th Annual Nepal AI School** in Kathmandu from **28 December 2026 to 7 January 2027**. It has around 45 hours of lectures and 12 hours of hands-on labs, on machine-learning foundations, geometric deep learning, neuro-symbolic AI, world models, generative modelling and AI for society ([NAAMII programme](https://www.nepalschool.naamii.org.np/program)). Check the official site for application categories and deadlines.

## How to choose a course

- **Free and official first:** short government and community courses are a low-risk way to start.
- **Check what is actually taught.** A real machine-learning course covers Python, maths and building models, not only using chatbots.
- **Learn the foundations yourself, for free:** the [Hamropedia AI & ML roadmap](/ai-roadmap/) lists 370+ free resources, in order.

All of this fits the direction set by [Nepal's National AI Policy](/nepal-national-ai-policy-2082-explained/), which calls for AI literacy for everyone from school students to the general public.
