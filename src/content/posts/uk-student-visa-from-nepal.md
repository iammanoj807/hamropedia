UK student visa rules have changed a lot since the government's May 2025 immigration white paper, and two more changes arrive in the next two months. This page covers the **UK student visa from Nepal** in 2026: the requirements, the **UK student visa fee**, the money you must show, and the rules that actually apply, separates them from what was only proposed, and gives each number with its official source. It also works out the cost in Nepali rupees for students applying from Nepal.

> **Update (5 November 2026):** the money you must show goes up for applications made **on or after 30 November 2026**: **£1,203 a month** outside London and **£1,570 a month** in London ([UKCISA](https://www.ukcisa.org.uk/news/student-update-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-september-2026/)). And from **1 January 2027** the Graduate visa drops to **18 months** for most graduates ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa)).

## UK student visa rules 2026: the short version

| Rule | What applies | Since |
|---|---|---|
| Student visa application fee | **£558** | 8 April 2026 |
| Immigration Health Surcharge (students) | **£776 per year** | 6 February 2024 |
| Money to show, outside London | **£1,203 per month**, up to 9 months (£1,171 before 30 Nov 2026) | 30 November 2026 |
| Money to show, London | **£1,570 per month**, up to 9 months (£1,529 before 30 Nov 2026) | 30 November 2026 |
| Graduate visa length (bachelor's, master's) | **2 years** if you apply on or before 31 Dec 2026; **18 months** from 1 Jan 2027 | Confirmed Oct 2025 |
| Graduate visa, "high-compliance" universities | **2 years** even after January 2027 (Budget policy; university list not yet published) | Announced 28 Oct 2026 |
| Graduate visa (PhD) | 3 years (unchanged) | — |
| Bringing dependants | Only for postgraduate research courses (or some government-funded scholarships) | Courses starting from January 2024 |
| Working during term | Usually up to **20 hours a week** on a degree-level course | — |

## 1. How much money you need to show (bank balance)

You must show you can pay your course fees and living costs. For living costs, the amount depends on **when you apply**:

| Where you study | Apply before 30 Nov 2026 | Apply on or after 30 Nov 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Outside London | £1,171 a month | **£1,203 a month** |
| London | £1,529 a month | **£1,570 a month** |

You show this for up to **9 months**, plus any first-year tuition fees you haven't paid yet ([UKCISA, September 2026](https://www.ukcisa.org.uk/news/student-update-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-september-2026/); [gov.uk: Student visa, money](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/money)).

**The 28-day rule:** the money must be in the account for **at least 28 days in a row**, and the end of that 28-day period must be **within 31 days of the date you apply** ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/money)). If the balance dips below the amount for even one day, the 28 days start again.

**Example, applying from Nepal on or after 30 November 2026:** for a course outside London you need 9 × £1,203 = **£10,827** for living costs. At Nepal Rastra Bank's selling rate on 5 November 2026 (NPR 204.71 to £1), that is about **NPR 22.2 lakh**, plus your unpaid tuition fees. In London it is 9 × £1,570 = **£14,130**, about **NPR 28.9 lakh**. Rates change daily: check the [NRB exchange rate](https://www.nrb.org.np/forex/) when you prepare your statement.

## 2. UK student visa fee and health surcharge

- **Student visa fee:** **£558**, whether you apply from outside the UK or extend/switch inside the UK ([gov.uk: Student visa](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa)). It was £524 before 8 April 2026.
- **Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS):** **£776 per year** for students and their dependants, paid up front for the whole visa. A part year of 6 months or less costs half, £388 ([gov.uk: healthcare surcharge](https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application/how-much-pay)).

Your visa usually runs a few months beyond the end of your course, so a one-year master's often means paying the IHS for more than one year. For example, 1 year and 4 months is £776 + £388 = **£1,164**. Use the official [IHS calculator](https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application/how-much-pay) for your exact figure.

## 3. Total cost of a UK student visa from Nepal

For a typical one-year master's outside London, applying on or after 30 November 2026 (rupee figures at NPR 204.71 to £1, NRB selling rate on 5 November 2026):

| Item | GBP | About NPR |
|---|---|---|
| Visa application fee | £558 | 1.14 lakh |
| Health surcharge (1 year 4 months) | £1,164 | 2.38 lakh |
| **Visa + surcharge** | **£1,722** | **3.53 lakh** |
| Living costs to show in your account | £10,827 | 22.2 lakh |
| Unpaid first-year tuition | your CAS amount | — |

The TB test, document translation and courier costs come on top. To send tuition fees or living costs out of Nepal, you also need a [No Objection Certificate (NOC)](/how-to-apply-noc-online-easy-steps-for-nepali-students/) from the education ministry.

## 4. UK student visa requirements for Nepalese students

- **A CAS** (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) from a licensed university or college.
- **Money** in your account for 28 days, as above.
- **English at CEFR level B2** for a degree-level course (B1 below degree level), shown through an approved test or your university's own assessment, depending on your sponsor ([gov.uk: knowledge of English](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/knowledge-of-english)).
- **A TB test certificate.** Nepal is on the list of countries that need one for visas of more than 6 months ([gov.uk: TB test](https://www.gov.uk/tb-test-visa/countries-where-you-need-a-tb-test-to-enter-the-uk)). The certificate lasts **6 months** from your chest X-ray, and must come from an approved clinic in Nepal.
- **Your academic documents**, as listed on your CAS.
- **Preparation for a [credibility interview](/top-ukvi-interview-question-examples-and-answers/)**: universities and UKVI often interview Nepali applicants about their course, finances and plans.

**When to apply:** from outside the UK, as early as **6 months** before your course starts. A decision usually takes about **3 weeks** ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa)).

## 5. The Graduate visa is getting shorter

The **Graduate visa** lets you stay and work in the UK after a UK degree, without a job offer. It lasts **2 years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026**, and **18 months if you apply on or after 1 January 2027**. PhD graduates still get 3 years. You must apply from inside the UK, and it costs **£937** plus the health surcharge ([gov.uk: Graduate visa](https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa)).

The October 2026 Budget says graduates of **"high-compliance" universities** will keep two years, but no list has been published. Full details: [UK Graduate visa: 18 months or 2 years?](/uk-graduate-visa-two-years-high-compliance-universities/)

## 6. Dependants

For courses starting from **January 2024**, most international students **cannot bring family members** (dependants). The exceptions are students on **postgraduate research** programmes such as a PhD, and some government-funded scholarship holders ([gov.uk: Student visa, family members](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/family-members)).

## 7. Working while you study

On a degree-level course you can usually work up to **20 hours a week during term time**, and full time during official vacations, unless your CAS or visa says otherwise ([gov.uk: Student visa](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa)).

## Other changes from the September 2026 rules

The Statement of Changes of 3 September 2026 also brought in, from **8 October 2026**, stricter deadlines for giving your **biometrics** and new rules on **fee waiver** dates (UKCISA). The UK also rejoins **Erasmus+** from 2027.

## What was proposed but is not (yet) a student rule

The [May 2025 immigration white paper](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/restoring-control-over-the-immigration-system-white-paper) proposed several other changes, including:

- **stricter compliance thresholds** for universities that sponsor international students;
- a **levy on universities' income from international students**;
- making **10 years**, rather than 5, the normal qualifying period for settlement (permanent residence). **Update (2 November 2026):** the government has now confirmed this, starting from December 2026 and through 2027. Student and Graduate visa time will not count towards settlement. See [UK ILR changes: 5-year vs 10-year routes](/ilr-changes-uk-5-year-vs-10-year-route-earned-settlement/).

Some of these need further legislation or consultation, and details change. **Treat them as proposals until they appear in the official Immigration Rules or on gov.uk.**

## Frequently asked questions

**What are the new UK student visa rules for 2026?** The main changes are higher money requirements from 30 November 2026 (£1,203 a month outside London, £1,570 in London), the £558 visa fee from April 2026, and the Graduate visa dropping to 18 months for applications from 1 January 2027.

**How much bank balance is required for a UK student visa from Nepal?** Your unpaid first-year tuition plus 9 months of living costs. Applying on or after 30 November 2026, that is £10,827 outside London (about NPR 22.2 lakh) or £14,130 in London (about NPR 28.9 lakh), held for 28 days in a row.

**How much does a UK student visa cost from Nepal?** The visa fee is £558 (about NPR 1.14 lakh). With the health surcharge, a one-year master's usually costs about £1,722 (about NPR 3.5 lakh) before living costs and tuition.

**Is IELTS compulsory for a UK student visa?** No single test is compulsory. You need English at B2 level for a degree course; many universities accept IELTS or another approved test, and some assess you themselves. Check what your university's CAS says.

**How much GPA is required to study in the UK from Nepal?** The visa has no GPA rule. Each university sets its own entry grades, so check your course page.

**Can I bring my husband or wife on a UK student visa?** Only if you are on a postgraduate research course such as a PhD, or on some government-funded scholarships.

**What about the Graduate visa after my course?** See [UK Graduate visa: 18 months or 2 years?](/uk-graduate-visa-two-years-high-compliance-universities/) for the dates, fee and exceptions.
