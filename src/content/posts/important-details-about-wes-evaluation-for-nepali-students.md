If you are applying to a university in the **USA or Canada**, sooner or later someone will ask for a "WES evaluation". This guide explains what a WES evaluation is, the difference between **WES ICAP and Basic** and between **course-by-course and document-by-document**, and what it costs in Nepal, using the fees published by WES itself (checked on 5 November 2026).

## What is WES?

**World Education Services (WES)** is a non-profit organisation that evaluates academic qualifications earned in one country and explains what they are equivalent to in another, mainly the USA and Canada. A WES report turns your marks, grades and credits into terms an admissions officer understands, such as a US grade point average (GPA) and US semester credits.

## Why universities ask for it

1. **To compare systems.** Grading and credit systems differ from country to country (Nepal's, for example, differ from North America's). The evaluation converts them into a familiar format.
2. **To verify documents.** WES checks that your documents are authentic, which is why transcripts usually have to reach WES in a sealed envelope from your university.
3. **Because it is required.** Many US and Canadian institutions list WES (or another accepted evaluator) as an admission requirement for international applicants.

> **Important:** Not every university accepts WES, and some want their own evaluation or a different evaluator. **Always check what your chosen university asks for before you pay.**

## Course-by-course vs document-by-document evaluation

WES offers two main evaluation types:

| | Document-by-Document | Course-by-Course |
|---|---|---|
| What it shows | Your qualifications and their equivalents | Every course, with credits, grade and level |
| GPA calculated | No | Yes (US 4.0 scale) |
| Commonly requested for | Some undergraduate admissions, some employers | Graduate admissions, licensing boards |
| Price (WES ICAP) | $171 | $239 |

**Rule of thumb:** if you are applying for a master's or PhD, or your university mentions GPA, you most likely need **course-by-course**. But the university's own instructions always win.

## WES ICAP vs WES Basic: what's the difference?

You also choose a service level. The short answer: **ICAP includes your verified transcripts with the report; Basic is the report only.**

- **WES ICAP (International Credential Advantage Package)** stores your verified transcripts and sends them, with your report, to the institutions you choose. WES labels it **Recommended**, and many universities require it because it includes verified transcripts.
- **WES Basic** gives you the evaluation report only. It is cheaper but does not include the verified transcripts, and sending to more institutions later is less convenient.

| | WES ICAP | WES Basic |
|---|---|---|
| Evaluation report | Yes | Yes |
| Verified transcripts sent with the report | **Yes** | No |
| Stored for sending to more institutions later | Yes | Report only |
| Course-by-course price | $239 | $186 |
| Document-by-document price | $171 | $118 |

If you choose Basic, WES lets you upgrade to ICAP for **$53** while the evaluation is still in progress, or **$92** after it is completed ([WES](https://www.wes.org/evaluations-and-fees/education/graduate-admissions/)). **Which should you choose?** ICAP, unless your university says Basic is fine: it costs $53 more, and you avoid ordering transcripts from Nepal a second time.

## WES evaluation cost in Nepal (fees for the USA)

These are the base fees WES lists for US graduate admissions ([WES fees page](https://www.wes.org/evaluations-and-fees/education/graduate-admissions/), checked 5 November 2026). Rupee amounts use Nepal Rastra Bank's US dollar selling rate on 5 November 2026, **NPR 151.73**:

| Evaluation | WES ICAP | WES Basic |
|---|---|---|
| Course-by-Course | **$239** (about NPR 36,300) | $186 (about NPR 28,200) |
| Document-by-Document | **$171** (about NPR 25,900) | $118 (about NPR 17,900) |

On top of the base fee:

- **Delivery of your report:** standard delivery **$14** (no tracking), or international courier **$86** per address.
- **Extra copies:** each additional report ordered with your application costs **$58**.
- **Sending your documents to WES** (courier from Nepal) is a separate cost you pay to the courier, and your university may charge for sealed transcripts.

**Typical total for a master's applicant from Nepal:** course-by-course ICAP ($239) + standard delivery ($14) = **$253, about NPR 38,400**, plus the courier from Nepal and your university's transcript fee. You pay WES online by card, so you need a card that works for dollar payments.

> **Note:** WES updates its fees from time to time, and Canadian evaluations (for example the ECA for immigration) have their own price list. Treat these numbers as a guide and confirm the total on wes.org before paying.

## How long does WES evaluation take?

WES starts only after **all** your official documents have arrived. Its published estimates ([WES processing times](https://www.wes.org/processing-times/)):

| Evaluation | Document verification | Evaluation |
|---|---|---|
| Course-by-Course | about 2 weeks | about 2 weeks (up to 4 if complex) |
| Document-by-Document | about 2 weeks | about 1 week (up to 2 if complex) |
| ECA for Canadian immigration | about 2 weeks | about 1 week (up to 2 if complex) |

Add the time for your university to prepare sealed transcripts and for the courier to reach WES. From Nepal, plan on **six to eight weeks in total** and start early.

## WES fees for Canada (ECA for immigration)

For Canadian immigration (Express Entry), you need WES's **Educational Credential Assessment (ECA)**: **C$264**. That includes an electronic report that IRCC can access, an official paper report and storage of your verified transcripts. Delivery is extra (**C$14** standard, **C$97** courier per address), and **13% HST** is added on top ([WES: ECA](https://www.wes.org/eca-2/)).

## How WES calculates your GPA

A course-by-course report converts each of your Nepali grades into a US grade and works out a **GPA on a 4.0 scale**. WES uses its own conversion for each country and institution, so a simple "divide by 25" formula often gives a different answer. For a free estimate before you pay, use WES's own [iGPA Calculator](https://apps.wes.org/igpa-calculator/); the GPA on your final report is what counts.

## Is a WES evaluation valid for 5 years?

The **5-year rule is for Canadian immigration**. For Express Entry, your Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) must be **less than 5 years old** both when you create your profile and when you apply ([IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/education-assessed/how.html)). For university admission, each university decides how old a report it accepts.

**WES or ICAS?** For Canadian immigration, IRCC accepts both: WES and the International Credential Assessment Service of Canada (ICAS) are both designated organisations, along with a few others (IRCC). For admission, follow your university's list.

## Which documents WES needs from your university

Usually:

- **Your degree certificate** (final or provisional), which you upload yourself as a clear copy.
- **Your official transcripts**, which normally have to be **sent to WES by your university in a sealed, signed or stamped envelope**, with your WES reference number on it.

WES shows the exact list for your country and qualification after you create your application, under **Required documents**. Follow that list exactly; it is what your evaluation will be checked against.

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Choose course-by-course (most graduate programmes) or document-by-document.
- [ ] Choose ICAP unless your university says Basic is fine.
- [ ] Have a card that can pay in US dollars.
- [ ] Budget for the base fee, delivery, extra recipients and courier from Nepal.
- [ ] Apply early: getting sealed transcripts from your university can take weeks.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference between WES ICAP and WES Basic?** ICAP sends your verified transcripts along with the evaluation report, and keeps them on file for later. Basic is the report only. ICAP costs $53 more.

**How much does a WES evaluation cost in Nepal?** Course-by-course ICAP is $239 (about NPR 36,300 on 5 November 2026), plus $14 standard delivery, the courier from Nepal and your university's transcript fee.

**What does ICAP mean in WES?** International Credential Advantage Package.

**How long does WES ICAP take?** About 2 weeks to verify your documents and about 2 weeks to evaluate a course-by-course report, after all documents arrive.

**Can I get a WES evaluation for free?** No. Every WES evaluation has a fee. Some universities accept other evaluators or evaluate transcripts themselves, so check before you pay.

**Course-by-course or document-by-document for a master's in the USA?** Usually course-by-course, because it includes a GPA. Follow your university's instructions.
