If you are applying to a university in the **USA or Canada**, sooner or later someone will ask for a "WES evaluation". This guide explains what it is, which type you need, and how much it costs, using the fees published by WES itself.

## What is WES?

**World Education Services (WES)** is a non-profit organisation that evaluates academic qualifications earned in one country and explains what they are equivalent to in another, mainly the USA and Canada. A WES report turns your Nepali marks, grades and credits into terms an admissions officer understands, such as a US grade point average (GPA) and US semester credits.

## Why universities ask for it

1. **To compare systems.** Nepal's grading and credit systems differ from North America's. The evaluation converts them into a familiar format.
2. **To verify documents.** WES checks that your documents are authentic, which is why transcripts usually have to reach WES in a sealed envelope from your university.
3. **Because it is required.** Many US and Canadian institutions list WES (or another accepted evaluator) as an admission requirement for international applicants.

> **Important:** Not every university accepts WES, and some want their own evaluation or a different evaluator. **Always check what your chosen university asks for before you pay.**

## Course-by-course vs document-by-document

WES offers two main evaluation types:

| | Document-by-Document | Course-by-Course |
|---|---|---|
| What it shows | Your qualifications and their equivalents | Every course, with credits, grade and level |
| GPA calculated | No | Yes (US 4.0 scale) |
| Commonly requested for | Some undergraduate admissions, some employers | Graduate admissions, licensing boards |
| Price (WES ICAP) | $171 | $239 |

**Rule of thumb:** if you are applying for a master's or PhD, or your university mentions GPA, you most likely need **course-by-course**. But the university's own instructions always win.

## WES ICAP vs WES Basic

You also choose a service level:

- **WES ICAP (International Credential Advantage Package)** stores your verified transcripts and sends them, with your report, to the institutions you choose. WES labels it **Recommended**, and many universities require it because it includes verified transcripts.
- **WES Basic** gives you the evaluation report only. It is cheaper but does not include the verified transcripts, and sending to more institutions later is less convenient.

If you choose Basic, WES lets you upgrade to ICAP for **$53** while the evaluation is still in progress.

## WES fees (USA)

These are the base fees WES lists on its [evaluations and fees page](https://www.wes.org/evaluations/), in US dollars:

| Evaluation | WES ICAP | WES Basic |
|---|---|---|
| Course-by-Course | **$239** | $186 |
| Document-by-Document | **$171** | $118 |

On top of the base fee:

- **Delivery:** standard delivery is listed at **$14** (without tracking); courier costs more.
- **Each additional institution** you send the report to costs extra.
- **Sending your documents to WES** (courier from Nepal) is a separate cost you pay to the courier.

> **Note:** WES updates its fees from time to time, and Canadian evaluations (for example the ECA for immigration) have their own price list. Treat these numbers as a guide and confirm the total on wes.org before paying.

## Which documents WES needs from a Nepali university

Usually:

- **Your degree certificate** (final or provisional), which you upload yourself as a clear copy.
- **Your official transcripts**, which normally have to be **sent to WES by your university in a sealed, signed or stamped envelope**, with your WES reference number on it.

WES shows the exact list for your country and qualification after you create your application, under **Required documents**. Follow that list exactly; it is what your evaluation will be checked against.

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Check which evaluator and which evaluation type your university requires.
- [ ] Choose course-by-course (most graduate programmes) or document-by-document.
- [ ] Choose ICAP unless your university says Basic is fine.
- [ ] Budget for the base fee, delivery, extra recipients and courier from Nepal.
- [ ] Apply early: getting sealed transcripts from a Nepali university can take weeks.
