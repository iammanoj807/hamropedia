Thousands of people in Nepal are searching for the **EDV 2027 opening date**. The short answer: **the DV Lottery 2027 (DV-2027, also called the green card lottery) entry form is not open**, and no new opening date has been announced. A US court had ordered the State Department to open it by **1 November 2026**, but on **29 October 2026** a US appeals court paused that order ([Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/united-states-ninth-circuit-stays-district-court-order-leaving-fy-2027-diversity-visa-lottery-program-halted-while-litigation-continues.html); [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/diversity-immigrant-visa-lottery-can-remain-paused-as-us-appeals)).

> **Latest, 5 November 2026:** still not open. No new date has been announced, and no further court ruling has been reported since the 29 October pause ([Envoy Global](https://www.envoyglobal.com/news-alert/u-s-diversity-visa-lottery-2027-program-remains-suspended-after-appeals-court-stay/); [Goeschl Law](https://www.golawcorp.com/news/ninth-circuit-stays-order-to-reopen-dv-2027-lottery-diversity-visa-program-remains-paused)). We update this page when anything changes.

> **At a glance**
>
> - **Is EDV 2027 open?** No. The DV-2027 registration has not opened anywhere in the world.
> - **Why not?** The US State Department paused the programme in December 2025, and a court order to reopen it was put on hold on 29 October 2026.
> - **What is the EDV 2027 opening date?** There isn't one yet. Watch only the official site, **dvprogram.state.gov**.
> - **New when it opens:** a **US$1** entry fee, and a **valid passport** for the main applicant.

## What happened to the DV lottery 2027, step by step

| Date | What happened |
|---|---|
| November 2025 | The State Department announces changes to the DV-2027 entry period |
| December 2025 | The State Department **pauses the Diversity Visa programme**, including DV-2026 visa processing |
| 19 August 2026 | Red Eagle Law, a US immigration law firm, sues over the failure to open DV-2027 |
| 14 October 2026 | A federal judge in California orders registration to open by **1 November 2026** for 30 days |
| 29 October 2026 | The **Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals pauses** that order; DV-2027 stays closed |

(Fragomen; Bloomberg Law)

The programme was suspended after a Portuguese national who had received a visa through the lottery was suspected of killing several people in shootings in Rhode Island and Massachusetts (Bloomberg Law).

The appeals court said "we do not endorse the Department's failure to open the lottery registration period", but found that the law firm that brought the case was unlikely to win, mainly because it is not a lottery applicant itself. The decision does **not** end the case or the programme: the appeal continues, and the situation could change (Bloomberg Law; Fragomen).

## What will be different when it opens

- **US$1 entry fee:** a new rule introduced a **US$1** electronic registration fee per entry, paid online when you submit ([Camp Legal](https://camplegal.com/understanding-the-dv-2027-lotterys-new-1-electronic-registration-fee/)). Entering used to be free.
- **Passport required:** under a final rule effective **10 April 2026**, the main applicant must give details of a **valid, unexpired passport** and upload a scan of its photo and signature page on the entry form ([EIG Law](https://eiglaw.com/dos-finalizes-new-passport-and-identity-requirements-for-diversity-visa-program/); [GT Law](https://www.gtlaw-insidebusinessimmigration.com/u-s-department-of-state-dos/state-department-final-rule-reinstates-passport-requirement-for-diversity-visa-lottery-entries-beginning-with-dv-2027/)).

## What to prepare now, before the form opens

When the entry period comes, it is usually only about a month long, and the last days are busy. Get these ready now:

1. **A valid passport** for the main applicant. If you don't have one, apply now: getting a Nepali passport takes time.
2. **A recent digital photo of every person on the entry**: you, your spouse and each unmarried child under 21, even if they won't move with you. The photo must be a JPEG of **240 kB or less**, square, and recent; use the State Department's [photo requirements](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/photos.html). Our free [DV photo tool](/tools/dv-photo-tool/) crops it to 600×600 pixels and keeps it under 240 kB, on your phone. Don't reuse an old photo.
3. **Proof you qualify**: a high school education or its equivalent, or qualifying work experience ([USA.gov](https://www.usa.gov/dv-lottery-eligibility)). The State Department has long defined this as a 12-year course of formal schooling, so in Nepal plan on having finished **Class 12 (+2)**, not only SEE. Check the DV-2027 instructions when they are published.
4. **A way to pay US$1 online**, such as a dollar card from your bank.
5. **Family details**: full names, dates of birth (convert BS dates to AD with our [Nepali date converter](/tools/nepali-date-converter/)), and place of birth for everyone on the entry.
6. **An email address you control**, not an agent's.

Official times will be given in US Eastern Time. Nepal is 10 hours 45 minutes ahead of US Eastern Time from November to March, and 9 hours 45 minutes ahead while the US is on daylight saving time.

## How to check the EDV result (there is no Nepal name list)

Every year, posts claiming to show an "EDV result Nepal name list" go viral. **There is no official name list**, for Nepal or any other country:

- Results are only on the official site, **dvprogram.state.gov**, under **Entrant Status Check**. You check them yourself with your **confirmation number** ([USA.gov](https://www.usa.gov/dv-lottery-results)).
- The State Department **does not tell winners by letter or email**, and it won't ask you to pay before you apply ([USA.gov](https://www.usa.gov/dv-lottery-results)).
- **Lost your confirmation number?** The official site has a way to retrieve it. Don't pay an agent to "find" your result.

Results for DV-2027 can only come after the entry period opens and closes, so any "EDV 2027 result" you see now is fake.

## How to avoid EDV scams

- The **only** place to enter is the official site, **dvprogram.state.gov**. Any other website or Facebook page saying "EDV 2027 is open" is wrong until that site says so.
- Nobody can register you early, and no agent can improve your chances. Selection is random.
- The official fee is **US$1**. Don't pay anyone for "guaranteed" or "priority" entries.
- When you enter, save your **confirmation number** yourself. You need it to check your result, so don't leave it only with an agent.
- Keep your photo and details exactly correct: one entry per person; duplicate entries are disqualified.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is EDV 2027 open in Nepal?** No. As of 5 November 2026, DV-2027 registration has not opened anywhere in the world.

**What is the EDV 2027 opening date in Nepal?** No date has been announced. It depends on the court case and the US State Department. When a date is announced, it applies worldwide, including Nepal.

**What happened to the DV lottery 2027?** The State Department paused the programme in December 2025. A judge ordered it to open by 1 November 2026, but an appeals court put that order on hold on 29 October 2026 while the appeal continues.

**Where do I register for the DV lottery 2027?** Only at **dvprogram.state.gov**, once it opens. There is no other official form, app or website.

**Is there a fee for the DV-2027 lottery?** Yes, a new **US$1** registration fee per entry, paid online when you submit. Anyone charging more is charging for their own service, not the lottery.

**Who is eligible for the DV-2027 lottery? Can Nepalis apply?** You must be a native of an eligible country and have a high school education or its equivalent, or qualifying work experience (USA.gov). Nepal has been among the eligible countries in recent years; the official DV-2027 instructions, when published, will confirm the list.

**Is there an EDV result Nepal name list?** No. The State Department doesn't publish names. You can only check your own result at dvprogram.state.gov with your confirmation number.

**When will EDV 2027 results come out?** Not known, because entries have not opened. Results are checked only on the official Entrant Status Check with your confirmation number. Any DV-2027 visas must still be issued by **30 September 2027**, so a late lottery means less time for winners ([VisaVerge](https://www.visaverge.com/greencard/dv-2027-winners-have-less-than-a-year-to-secure-their-green-card-visas/)).

**Is the DV lottery open for 2028?** No. The programme is named after the US fiscal year of the visas. DV-2027 is the one currently delayed; no dates for DV-2028 have been announced.
