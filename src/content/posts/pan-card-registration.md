If you have a national ID, **PAN card registration** in Nepal no longer needs a trip to a tax office. On the Inland Revenue Department's (IRD) new taxpayer portal you open **Application For PPAN Registration**, enter the mobile number linked to your NID, confirm the OTP and your NID number, and IRD creates your **personal PAN card** online, ready for you to print. Here is **how to make a PAN card in Nepal**, step by step, with screenshots of the official pages. You'll need your NID number: the [NID card check](/nid-card-check/) shows how to find it. Looking up an existing PAN number instead? Use the [IRD PAN search guide](/ird-pan-search-nepal/).

## Who needs a PAN card?

Under IRD's **"PAN for all"** policy (<span lang="ne">सबैका लागि स्थायी लेखा नम्बर</span>), anyone who earns income during a fiscal year, whether a salary, allowance, wages or house rent, has to take a personal PAN ([IRD FAQ](https://ird.gov.np/faq/)).

## Online PAN card registration with your NID: step by step

IRD explains this route in its own video, <span lang="ne">"राष्ट्रिय परिचयपत्र नम्बरका आधारमा व्यक्तिगत स्थायी लेखा नम्बर स्वचालित रुपमा अनलाइन सिर्जना गर्ने"</span> (creating a personal PAN online automatically from your national ID number), posted on YouTube on 21 November 2025 ([IRD video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G0NMr6ZXvfI)). The steps below match that video and the live portal as we found it on 7 November 2026.

### Step 1: Open the new taxpayer portal and choose Application For PPAN Registration

From **ird.gov.np**, click **<span lang="ne">करदाता पोर्टल (नयाँ)</span>** (Taxpayer Portal, new), or go straight to [taxpayerportalb.ird.gov.np](https://taxpayerportalb.ird.gov.np/). In the menu, click **Registration(PAN, VAT, EXCISE)** (1) and then **Application For PPAN Registration** (2).

![IRD taxpayer portal menu with Registration (PAN, VAT, EXCISE) and Application For PPAN Registration marked](/images/posts/pan/pan-1-portal-menu.webp "Step 1: Registration(PAN, VAT, EXCISE), then Application For PPAN Registration")

### Step 2: Enter the mobile number registered with your NID

The page reads: "If you have a National ID(NID), enter the mobile number registered in the NID system to get your PAN." Enter that number (1) and the captcha (2), then click **Continue** (3).

![PAN registration page asking for the mobile number registered in the NID system, a captcha and the Continue button](/images/posts/pan/pan-2-nid-mobile.webp "Step 2: the mobile number registered in the NID system, the captcha, then Continue")

Use the number on your NID record, not just whichever phone you carry. If you've changed numbers, see the OTP question in the [FAQ](#frequently-asked-questions) below.

### Step 3: Type the OTP

An **OTP Verification** box asks you to "Enter OTP sent to your Mobile". Enter the code from the SMS and click **Continue**.

![OTP Verification box on the IRD taxpayer portal asking for the OTP sent to your mobile](/images/posts/pan/pan-4-otp.webp "Step 3: type the OTP from the SMS. Screenshot: IRD video, 21 November 2025 (personal details blurred)")

### Step 4: Choose your Inland Revenue Office and enter your NID number

Pick the **Inland Revenue Office** (IRO) that suits you from the **IRO Name** list. Under **National Identification Number (NIN)**, tick **YES** for "Do you have a National Identification Number (NIN)?" and enter your NIN, then click **Continue**.

![IRO Name list and the National Identification Number (NIN) question with YES ticked](/images/posts/pan/pan-5-iro-nin.webp "Step 4: choose your Inland Revenue Office, tick YES and enter your NIN. Screenshot: IRD video, 21 November 2025 (personal details blurred)")

The portal then tells you which office will keep your record, for example <span lang="ne">"तपाईंको यो विवरण आ.रा.का. विराटनगर मा दर्ता हुनेछ। के तपाईं अघि बढ्न चाहनुहुन्छ?"</span> (your details will be registered at the Biratnagar IRO; do you want to continue?). Click **Yes**.

### Step 5: Check the form tabs

![PAN registration form with the Personal Info, Address, Documents, Relatives, Financial Info and Preview & Submit tabs](/images/posts/pan/pan-6-form-tabs.webp "Step 5: the form tabs, starting with Personal Info. Screenshot: IRD video, 21 November 2025 (personal details blurred)")

The form has six tabs: **Personal Info**, **Address**, **Documents**, **Relatives**, **Financial Info** and **Preview & Submit**. Your NID record fills in a lot of it, so your job is mostly to check the details and add anything missing:

- **Personal Info:** your name in Nepali and English, gender and date of birth. To type your name in Nepali, use the [Nepali typing tool](/tools/english-to-nepali/) and type it by sound: `raam bahaadur thaapaa` gives राम बहादुर थापा.
- **Address:** your permanent address, with tick boxes to reuse it as your temporary or mailing address.
- **Documents:** your citizenship certificate details appear here.
- **Relatives:** add the names of family members.
- **Financial Info:** your source of income (employment, investment, business, occupation or others) and, optionally, your bank account.

IRD forms use Bikram Sambat dates. The [Nepali date converter](/tools/nepali-date-converter/) checks any BS date for you.

### Step 6: Preview and submit

On **Preview & Submit**, go through every detail once more. Tick the self-declaration that everything is true, click **Submit** and confirm.

### Step 7: Get your PAN and print the PAN card

A success message confirms that your registration is complete and your PAN has been created. It says the PAN will reach you by mobile and email, and that you can **print the card with "Print Card"**. Print it or save it as a PDF, and keep it with your documents.

## How to print or download your PAN card

Use **Print Card** to print or save the card straight away (Step 7). If you lose a personal PAN card later, IRD's FAQ says you can print it again from your **taxpayer login**, or apply at the office that holds your PAN ([IRD FAQ](https://ird.gov.np/faq/)).

## IRD PAN login: which taxpayer portal?

There are two taxpayer portals linked from ird.gov.np:

- **<span lang="ne">करदाता पोर्टल (नयाँ)</span>** (new): [taxpayerportalb.ird.gov.np](https://taxpayerportalb.ird.gov.np/), with **Application For PPAN Registration** (personal PAN with NID), **Application For Business Registration**, **Registration Login**, and returns such as VAT and income tax.
- **<span lang="ne">करदाता पोर्टल</span>** (older): [taxpayerportal.ird.gov.np](https://taxpayerportal.ird.gov.np/taxpayer/app.html), with Registration(PAN,VAT,EXCISE), VAT, Income Tax and e-TDS menus.

A new personal PAN with your NID is done on the new portal. That's also what people searching for "www ird gov np pan" are usually after.

## Business PAN registration in Nepal

A business gets its own PAN, separate from the owner's personal PAN. On the new portal, choose **Application For Business Registration**. IRD's [PAN forms page](https://ird.gov.np/category/pan/) has separate business PAN registration and amendment forms for:

- companies
- partnership firms
- joint ventures
- NGOs, charities, cooperatives and similar bodies
- withholding (With-Holder PAN)

Your Inland Revenue Office can tell you which documents your type of business needs.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I make a PAN card in Nepal?** With an NID, you register online. Open taxpayerportalb.ird.gov.np, choose Application For PPAN Registration, enter your NID mobile number, the OTP and your NID number, check the form and submit. IRD creates your PAN, and the Print Card button prints the card.

**Can I do PAN card registration online in Nepal?** Yes. IRD's new taxpayer portal creates a personal PAN online from your national ID number, using the mobile number registered with your NID for the OTP. IRD walked through the process in a video on 21 November 2025.

**What is the full form of PAN?** PAN stands for Permanent Account Number, स्थायी लेखा नम्बर in Nepali: the tax number the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) gives individuals and businesses. IRD calls a personal PAN "PPAN", and the PAN card is the printed card with your number on it.

**Can I get a PAN without an NID?** The online route depends on an NID and its registered mobile number. Without one, [register for your NID](/nid-card-check/#don-and-39-t-have-an-nid-yet-nid-pre-enrollment-and-registration) first, or ask your nearest Inland Revenue Office or Taxpayer Service Office. The personal PAN registration form is on IRD's [PAN forms page](https://ird.gov.np/category/pan/).

**Why am I not getting the OTP for PAN registration?** The OTP only goes to the mobile number registered in the NID system. If your NID has an old number or none at all, [update it at an NID registration unit](/nid-card-check/#no-otp-your-mobile-number-isn-and-39-t-linked-to-your-nid) and try again. Also check that you typed the number and the captcha correctly.

**Do I need to give bank details for a PAN?** No. The Financial Info tab says bank details aren't mandatory, but that they make the tax refund process easier. Add your bank, branch and account number if you want any refund paid straight into your account.
