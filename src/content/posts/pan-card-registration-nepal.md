**PAN card registration** in Nepal is now online for anyone with a national ID. On the Inland Revenue Department's (IRD) new taxpayer portal, choose **Application For PPAN Registration**, enter the mobile number linked to your NID, confirm the OTP and your NID number, and IRD creates your **personal PAN card** online. You can then print the card yourself. This guide shows **how to make a PAN card in Nepal** step by step, with screenshots of the official pages. Don't know your NID number? Find it with the [NID card check](/nid-card-check-nepal/). Want to check a PAN number instead? See the [IRD PAN search guide](/ird-pan-search/).

## Who needs a PAN card?

IRD's policy called **"PAN for all"** (<span lang="ne">सबैका लागि स्थायी लेखा नम्बर</span>) says that anyone who earns income in a fiscal year, such as a salary, allowance, wages or house rent, must take a personal PAN ([IRD FAQ](https://ird.gov.np/faq/)).

## Online PAN card registration with your NID: step by step

IRD's own video explains this new route: <span lang="ne">"राष्ट्रिय परिचयपत्र नम्बरका आधारमा व्यक्तिगत स्थायी लेखा नम्बर स्वचालित रुपमा अनलाइन सिर्जना गर्ने"</span> (creating a personal PAN online automatically from your national ID number). IRD posted it on YouTube on 21 November 2025 ([IRD video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G0NMr6ZXvfI)). The steps below follow that video and the live portal as it looked on 7 November 2026.

### Step 1: Open the new taxpayer portal and choose Application For PPAN Registration

Go to **ird.gov.np** and click **<span lang="ne">करदाता पोर्टल (नयाँ)</span>** (Taxpayer Portal, new), or open [taxpayerportalb.ird.gov.np](https://taxpayerportalb.ird.gov.np/) directly. In the menu, click **Registration(PAN, VAT, EXCISE)** (1), then **Application For PPAN Registration** (2).

![IRD taxpayer portal menu with Registration (PAN, VAT, EXCISE) and Application For PPAN Registration marked](/images/posts/pan/pan-1-portal-menu.webp "Step 1: Registration(PAN, VAT, EXCISE), then Application For PPAN Registration")

### Step 2: Enter the mobile number registered with your NID

The page says: "If you have a National ID(NID), enter the mobile number registered in the NID system to get your PAN." Type that mobile number (1) and the captcha (2), then click **Continue** (3).

![PAN registration page asking for the mobile number registered in the NID system, a captcha and the Continue button](/images/posts/pan/pan-2-nid-mobile.webp "Step 2: the mobile number registered in the NID system, the captcha, then Continue")

It must be the number on your NID record, not just any number you use. If you changed numbers, see the OTP question in the [FAQ](#frequently-asked-questions) below.

### Step 3: Type the OTP

An **OTP Verification** box asks you to "Enter OTP sent to your Mobile". Type the code from the SMS and click **Continue**.

![OTP Verification box on the IRD taxpayer portal asking for the OTP sent to your mobile](/images/posts/pan/pan-4-otp.webp "Step 3: type the OTP from the SMS. Screenshot: IRD video, 21 November 2025 (personal details blurred)")

### Step 4: Choose your Inland Revenue Office and enter your NID number

Choose the **Inland Revenue Office** (IRO) that is convenient for you from the **IRO Name** list. Under **National Identification Number (NIN)**, answer **YES** to "Do you have a National Identification Number (NIN)?" and type your NIN. Click **Continue**.

![IRO Name list and the National Identification Number (NIN) question with YES ticked](/images/posts/pan/pan-5-iro-nin.webp "Step 4: choose your Inland Revenue Office, tick YES and enter your NIN. Screenshot: IRD video, 21 November 2025 (personal details blurred)")

The portal then confirms which office will hold your record, for example: <span lang="ne">"तपाईंको यो विवरण आ.रा.का. विराटनगर मा दर्ता हुनेछ। के तपाईं अघि बढ्न चाहनुहुन्छ?"</span> (your details will be registered at the Biratnagar IRO; do you want to continue?). Click **Yes**.

### Step 5: Check the form tabs

![PAN registration form with the Personal Info, Address, Documents, Relatives, Financial Info and Preview & Submit tabs](/images/posts/pan/pan-6-form-tabs.webp "Step 5: the form tabs, starting with Personal Info. Screenshot: IRD video, 21 November 2025 (personal details blurred)")

The form opens with these tabs: **Personal Info**, **Address**, **Documents**, **Relatives**, **Financial Info**, and **Preview & Submit**. Much of it is already filled in from your NID record, so mostly you check the details and add what is missing:

- **Personal Info:** your name in Nepali and English, gender and date of birth. Need to type your name in Nepali? Use the [Nepali typing tool](/tools/english-to-nepali/): type it by sound, such as `raam bahaadur thaapaa` for राम बहादुर थापा.
- **Address:** permanent address, with tick boxes to copy it as your temporary or mailing address.
- **Documents:** your citizenship certificate details are listed here.
- **Relatives:** add family members' names.
- **Financial Info:** your source of income (employment, investment, business, occupation or others) and, if you like, your bank account.

Dates on IRD forms are in Bikram Sambat. To check a BS date, use the [Nepali date converter](/tools/nepali-date-converter/).

### Step 6: Preview and submit

On **Preview & Submit**, read everything once more. Tick the self-declaration that the details are true, then click **Submit** and confirm.

### Step 7: Get your PAN and print the PAN card

A success message says your registration is complete and your PAN has been created. It also says you will get the PAN on your mobile and email, and that you can **print the card with "Print Card"**. Print it or save it as a PDF and keep it with your documents.

## How to print or download your PAN card

Print or save the card with **Print Card** as soon as you finish (Step 7). If you lose a personal PAN card later, IRD's FAQ says you can print it again from your **taxpayer login**, or apply at the office that holds your PAN ([IRD FAQ](https://ird.gov.np/faq/)).

## IRD PAN login: which taxpayer portal?

ird.gov.np links two taxpayer portals:

- **<span lang="ne">करदाता पोर्टल (नयाँ)</span>** (new): [taxpayerportalb.ird.gov.np](https://taxpayerportalb.ird.gov.np/). It has **Application For PPAN Registration** (personal PAN with NID), **Application For Business Registration**, **Registration Login**, and returns such as VAT and income tax.
- **<span lang="ne">करदाता पोर्टल</span>** (older): [taxpayerportal.ird.gov.np](https://taxpayerportal.ird.gov.np/taxpayer/app.html), which also has Registration(PAN,VAT,EXCISE), VAT, Income Tax and e-TDS menus.

For a new personal PAN with your NID, use the new portal. When people search "www ird gov np pan", this is the place they want.

## Business PAN registration in Nepal

A business needs its own PAN, separate from your personal PAN. On the new portal, choose **Application For Business Registration**. IRD's [PAN forms page](https://ird.gov.np/category/pan/) has separate business PAN registration and amendment forms for:

- companies
- partnership firms
- joint ventures
- NGOs, charities, cooperatives and similar bodies
- withholding (With-Holder PAN)

Ask your Inland Revenue Office which documents your type of business needs.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I make a PAN card in Nepal?** If you have an NID, register online. Open taxpayerportalb.ird.gov.np, choose Application For PPAN Registration, enter your NID mobile number, the OTP and your NID number, check the form and submit. IRD creates your PAN, and you can print the card with the Print Card button.

**Can I do PAN card registration online in Nepal?** Yes. IRD's new taxpayer portal creates a personal PAN online from your national ID number. You need the mobile number registered with your NID for the OTP. IRD explained the process in a video on 21 November 2025.

**What is the full form of PAN?** PAN stands for Permanent Account Number, स्थायी लेखा नम्बर in Nepali. It is the tax number that the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) gives to individuals and to businesses. IRD calls a personal PAN "PPAN", and the PAN card is the printed card that shows your number.

**Can I get a PAN without an NID?** The online route needs an NID and its registered mobile number. Without one, [register for your NID](/nid-card-check-nepal/#don-and-39-t-have-an-nid-yet-nid-pre-enrollment-and-registration) first, or ask your nearest Inland Revenue Office or Taxpayer Service Office. IRD lists the personal PAN registration form on its [PAN forms page](https://ird.gov.np/category/pan/).

**Why am I not getting the OTP for PAN registration?** The OTP goes only to the mobile number registered in the NID system. If your NID has an old number or none, [update it at an NID registration unit](/nid-card-check-nepal/#no-otp-your-mobile-number-isn-and-39-t-linked-to-your-nid), then try again. Check that you typed the number and the captcha correctly.

**Do I need to give bank details for a PAN?** No. The Financial Info tab says bank details are not mandatory, but giving them makes the tax refund process easier. You can add your bank, branch and account number if you want refunds paid straight into your account.
